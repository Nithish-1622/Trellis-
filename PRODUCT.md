# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Trellis serves web-based, English-speaking learners with varied experience levels who are trying to reach a concrete career or skill goal. They need help turning an aspiration, prior learning, available time, and preferences into an achievable sequence of verified learning work.

## Product Purpose

Trellis is a personalized learning-path assistant. It captures a learner's goal and evidence, recommends real resources, builds a prerequisite-aware roadmap, explains its reasoning, tracks progress, and proposes adaptations that the learner explicitly approves. Success means a learner always understands what to do next, why it fits them, and how their evidence changed the plan.

## Brand Personality

Precise, encouraging, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a capable learning coach: direct about gaps and constraints, calm when something fails, and respectful of learner agency.

## Anti-references

Trellis must not resemble a generic AI chat wrapper, an NFT or speculative product, a decorative purple-gradient dashboard, or a course marketplace that presents an unranked wall of cards. It must never imply that invented links, opaque AI judgments, or silent roadmap changes are trustworthy.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious without hiding the learner's wider plan.
- Explain recommendations with evidence and provenance, not AI authority.
- Preserve learner agency: drafts resume, edits remain possible, and adaptations require confirmation.
- Ask only for information that materially improves the plan and mark optional input clearly.
- Treat failure as recoverable: persist progress before expensive generation and provide a clear retry path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA across keyboard navigation, focus visibility, labels, contrast, responsive reflow, screen-reader semantics, and reduced motion. Accessibility preferences are learner profile inputs, not afterthoughts. The pilot is English-language, but copy and layouts should tolerate longer future translations.
